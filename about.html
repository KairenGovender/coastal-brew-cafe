About Us

Passionate about coffee, community, and quality.

Our Story

Coastal Brew Café opened its doors in 2023 with a simple mission: to serve the best coffee in Durban. Founded by a team of passionate baristas, the café quickly became a favourite spot for locals and visitors alike.

What started as a dream shared by two coffee enthusiasts has grown into a beloved community hub.

Our Mission

To create a welcoming space where every cup tells a story and every customer feels like family.

Our Vision

To become Durban's most loved coffee destination, known for quality, consistency, and community spirit.

Our Core Values

Quality First

We source the finest beans and ingredients, ensuring every cup and bite is exceptional.

Community Focused

We're proud to be part of Durban's vibrant community and support local initiatives.

Sustainable Practices

We're committed to reducing our environmental footprint through ethical sourcing.
